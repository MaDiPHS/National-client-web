What is MaDiPHS CKAN?

The Malawi Digital Plant Health Service (MaDiPHS) CKAN is an open-source data management system designed to facilitate the collection, management, and dissemination of agricultural data in Malawi. Hosted at https://ckan.madiphs.org/, it serves as a centralized repository for datasets related to agronomy, crop pests and diseases, landforms, soil conditions, weeds, weather, and other agricultural knowledge resources.

Features of CKAN
Data Cataloging

MaDiPHS CKAN organizes datasets and knowledge products, making them easily searchable through metadata and tags such as pests, diseases, and crops.

Accessibility

By adhering to FAIR (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, and Reusable) data principles, it ensures that agricultural data is readily available.

Integration

The platform integrates data from various sources, including national, regional, and global inputs, for comprehensive information.

How to Use MaDiPHS CKAN
1
Browsing Datasets

Users can explore available datasets by visiting the MaDiPHS Knowledge Library. The platform provides filters and search functionalities to locate specific data based on keywords, tags, or organizations.

2
Accessing Data

Once a dataset of interest is identified, users can view detailed metadata, including descriptions, formats, and usage rights. Many datasets are available for direct download.

3
Contributing Data

Organizations and researchers can contribute their datasets to the platform, enhancing the repository's richness and supporting collaborative efforts.

You can read more about CKAN on https://ckan.madiphs.org/
