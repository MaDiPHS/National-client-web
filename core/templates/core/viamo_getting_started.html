MaDiPHS Viamo 321

Agricultural information and plant health services through mobile voice technology

Dial 321
Target Audience

The MaDiPHS Viamo 321 service is designed to provide accessible agricultural and plant health information to farmers, agricultural extension workers, researchers, agribusinesses, and communities across Malawi. The service is particularly useful for users who may have limited access to smartphones, internet connectivity, or agricultural information resources.

Farmers Extension Workers Researchers Agribusiness Communities
What is MaDiPHS Viamo 321?

MaDiPHS Viamo 321 is a mobile agricultural information service designed to make important plant health and agricultural knowledge accessible to people across Malawi through a simple telephone-based service.

Users can access agricultural information using a mobile phone without requiring a smartphone or internet connection. The service uses interactive voice technology to deliver information on crops, pests, diseases, farming practices, and other agricultural topics.

Simple access

Dial 321 from your mobile phone and follow the voice prompts to access available agricultural information.

Why Viamo 321 for MaDiPHS?
No Internet Required

Farmers can access information through a mobile phone without depending on mobile internet.

Accessible Information

Voice-based information can make agricultural knowledge easier to access for communities with different literacy levels.

National Reach

A mobile telephone service provides an opportunity to extend agricultural information beyond physical information centres.

Plant Health Focus

MaDiPHS use the service to disseminate practical knowledge relating to crop pests, diseases, and plant health.

MaDiPHS as the National Agricultural Client

As the national client for agricultural and plant health information, MaDiPHS use Viamo 321 as an additional channel for reaching farmers and agricultural stakeholders throughout Malawi. Information developed and validated through the MaDiPHS knowledge ecosystem can be adapted into short, practical voice messages suitable for mobile delivery.
