ABOUT NATIONAL CLIENT

MaDiPHS National Client - Digital Plant Health Platform

The MaDiPHS National Client is a national-level digital platform that enables Malawi to access and operationalize plant health information through the MaDiPHS International Integration Platform, integrating weather data, satellite observations, pest surveillance, and decision support systems.

  • Data-Driven Insights

    Transforming complex agricultural data into actionable intelligence

  • Farmer-Centric Solutions

    Supporting evidence-based decision making for food security

  • Integration of multiple national, regional, and global data sources

  • Early warning systems for pest and disease outbreaks

  • Web-based dashboards and digital tools for stakeholders
